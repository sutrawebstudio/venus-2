FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Help & Policies

How do I exchange sizes if it does not fit?

We provide a 7-day doorstep size replacement. Simply contact our support team with your Order ID, and our courier partner will pick up the existing pair and deliver your requested size free of charge.

What are the shipping charges?

Shipping is completely FREE across India for all prepaid orders above ₹499. Orders below ₹499 incur a nominal ₹49 delivery charge.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched directly from our factory within 24 hours. Metros receive delivery within 2–4 business days; rest of India within 4–6 days.

Are Venus footwear water-resistant?

Yes! Our PU Chappals, EVA Clogs, and PVC rainwear ranges are engineered specifically for Indian weather, water exposure, and easy washability.
