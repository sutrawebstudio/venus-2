OUR MILESTONES

The Venus Journey

A legacy built on vulcanized rubber, PU injection technology, and trusted family relationships.

1982

The Origin of Venus Footwear

Started manufacturing traditional virgin rubber Hawai chappals with a vision to deliver footwear that never cracks in summer heat or slips on monsoon clay.

1996

Automated Direct PU Injection

Pioneered high-density direct PU injection technology in North India, creating anatomical footbeds that provide orthopedic heel relief to daily wage earners and shopkeepers.

2006

Incorporation of Venus Footarts Limited

Formalized into a limited enterprise with expanded plant infrastructure, establishing dedicated labs for flex-resistance, anti-skid testing, and raw material purity.

2018

Global Export & EVA UltraLite Expansion

Launched featherlight EVA closed-cell clogs and expanded export footprint across Middle East, Southeast Asia, and East Africa.

2026

Next-Gen Direct-to-Consumer (D2C)

Bringing direct factory pricing, personalized size fittings, and honest guarantees directly to millions of Indian consumers online.
